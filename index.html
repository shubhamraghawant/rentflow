📲 Install RentFlow on Your Phone

RentFlow installs directly onto your phone without requiring app store downloads:

🤖 For Android (Chrome / Brave):

  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) in Chrome at the top right.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap Install. RentFlow will appear as an app on your phone!

🍎 For iPhone (Safari):

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (⎙ / square with arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top right.
